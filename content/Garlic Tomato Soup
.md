# Description
This garlic tomato soup is a savory and flavorful dish that is perfect for a chilly day. It's made with fresh garlic, tomatoes, and a variety of seasonings, and is topped with a sprinkle of parmesan and fresh basil.

---
# Ingredients
## Roast
- [ ] 2 Whole Heads **Garlic**
- [ ] 2 Lbs Roma **tomatoes**
- [ ] 4 Whole **Shallots** (2 Small Yellow **Onions**)
- [ ] 1 Tbsp Coarse **Salt**
- [ ] 2 Tsp Ground **Black Pepper**
- [ ] 1 tsp **Oregano**
- [ ] 1/3 Cup **Olive oil**
- [ ] 11/2 Tsp **Red Chili Flakes**
## Soup Base
- [ ] 1/3 cup **Sherry** cooking wine
- [ ] 2 Cups **Chicken Broth**
- [ ] 1 Tbsp **Tomato Paste**
- [ ] 2 Tbsp Chopped **Basil**
- [ ] 1 Tsp **Onion Powder**
- [ ] 1 Tsp **Garlic Powder**
- [ ] 1/2 Tsp **Italian Seasoning**
- [ ] 3 Tbsp **Salted Butter**
- [ ] 1/2 Cup Fresh **Parmesan**
- [ ] Fresh **Basil** (for garnish)
- [ ] **Parmesan** (for garnish)

---
# Method
1. In a large pot, heat **olive oil** over medium heat.
2. Add **garlic** and **shallots** and cook until fragrant, about 2 minutes.
3. Add **tomatoes**, **salt**, **black pepper**, **oregano**, and **red chili flakes**. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Pour in **sherry** cooking wine and let it cook for 2 minutes.
5. Add **chicken broth**, **tomato paste**, **basil**, **onion powder**, **garlic powder**, and **italian seasoning**. Stir to combine.
6. Let the soup simmer for 15 minutes.
7. In a separate pan, melt **butter** over medium heat.
8. Add **parmesan** and stir until melted and combined.
9. Pour the melted **butter** and **parmesan** mixture into the soup and stir to combine.
10. Let the soup simmer for an additional 5 minutes.
