# Description
German side dishes that quietly appears at almost every summer BBQ. Nothing fancy. Just comforting, fresh and made to share. If you grew up in Germany, there's a good chance this was sitting somewhere on the table next to grilled meat, bread and garden salads.

---
# Ingredients
- [ ] 1 kg waxy potatoes
- [ ] 1 cucumber
- [ ] 1 small red onion or shallot
- [ ] fresh chives or dill
Dressing:
- [ ] 5 tbsp light vinegar (I used a mild herb vinegar)
- [ ] 6 tbsp oil (traditionally a neutral oil, but I used olive oil)
- [ ] 1 tsp mustard
- [ ] 1 tsp sugar
- [ ] 100 ml warm vegetable broth
- [ ] salt & black pepper

---
# Method
1. **Boil the potatoes** until fork tender.
2. **Slice** while still warm.
3. **Thinly slice** the **cucumber** and **onions**.
4. **Warm the broth** with **vinegar**, **mustard**, **sugar**, **salt** and **pepper**.
5. **Pour** over the warm potatoes and **let soak**.
6. Add **cucumber**, **onions** and **herbs**.
