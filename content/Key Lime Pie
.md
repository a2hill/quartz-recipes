# Description
A homemade graham cracker crust filled with a creamy, tart and sweet key lime filling topped with vanilla bean whipped cream.

---
# Ingredients
- [ ] 12 graham crackers
- [ ] 7 tbsp melted butter
- [ ] 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- [ ] 4 oz softened cream cheese
- [ ] 3 egg yolks
- [ ] 14 oz sweetened condensed milk
- [ ] 1/2 cup heavy whipping cream
- [ ] 1/2 cup key lime juice
- [ ] 3/4 cup heavy whipping cream
- [ ] 1/2 cup powdered sugar
- [ ] 1/2 tsp vanilla bean paste

---
# Method
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. In a food processor, crush the **graham crackers** until they are fine crumbs.
3. In a mixing bowl, combine the **graham cracker crumbs**, **melted butter**, and **granulated sugar**. Mix until well combined.
4. Press the mixture into a 9-inch pie dish, making sure to press it evenly on the bottom and up the sides.
5. Bake the crust for 10 minutes, then let it cool completely.
6. In a separate mixing bowl, beat the **softened cream cheese** until smooth.
7. Add in the **egg yolks** one at a time, mixing well after each addition.
8. Gradually add in the **sweetened condensed milk**, mixing until well combined.
9. Slowly pour in the **heavy whipping cream** and **key lime juice**, mixing until the filling is smooth.
10. Pour the filling into the cooled crust and bake for 15 minutes.
11. Let the pie cool completely, then refrigerate for at least 2 hours.
12. In a mixing bowl, beat the **heavy whipping cream** until soft peaks form.
13. Gradually add in the **powdered sugar** and **vanilla bean paste**, beating until stiff peaks form.
