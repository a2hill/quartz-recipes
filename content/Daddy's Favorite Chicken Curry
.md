# Description
This chicken curry recipe is a favorite of the author's father. It is a flavorful and creamy dish that can serve 4 people, or 2 with leftovers for another meal.

---
# Ingredients
- [ ] 1¼ cups finely chopped onions
- [ ] 2 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] ½ inch piece of ginger, chopped
- [ ] 1 hot green chile or Serrano pepper, seeds and pith removed (optional)
- [ ] 2 tbsp mustard oil or grapeseed oil (vegetable oil will also work)
- [ ] ½ tsp cumin seeds
- [ ] 1 cup diced or crushed tomatoes, canned or fresh
- [ ] 2 tbsp Indian or Greek yogurt (preferably not fat free)
- [ ] 2 tbsp sour cream
- [ ] 1 tsp ground coriander
- [ ] ½ tsp ground cumin
- [ ] ½ tsp turmeric
- [ ] ¼ tsp chile (cayenne) powder
- [ ] ¼ tsp ground black pepper
- [ ] ½ tsp garam masala
- [ ] 1 tsp Kasoori Methi (dried fenugreek leaves; omit if unavailable)
- [ ] 1 tsp Kosher salt
- [ ] 2 lbs bone-in, skin-on (highly preferable) or boneless skinless chicken thighs
- [ ] 1 tbsp lemon juice
- [ ] 2 tbsp fresh chopped cilantro
- [ ] 2 tbsp heavy cream
- [ ] 1½ – 2 cups chicken stock or broth (preferable) or warm water
- [ ] 6 or 7 small new potatoes (optional)

# Method

Don't forget about things like **salt** **pepper** and **seasonings** which may not have been listed in the ingredients --> 
1. If you are using bone-in skin- on chicken thighs, remove the skin and most of the skin from each thigh, then cut it into bite size pieces as follows: trim off the boneless portion and cut it into bite size pieces if large enough. Cut the bone-in portion in half so that the bone is cut in half. Use a cleaver or a large kitchen knife to do this: set the knife on the **chicken** and then hit the top the blade hard with the palm of your hand. Set the **chicken** aside.
2. If you are using potatoes, peel them and cut each potato in half if they are large enough; otherwise leave them whole. Heat enough oil in a small saucepan so that the depth of oil is enough to cover the potatoes. When the oil is hot enough (about 350°F) add the **potatoes** and fry them until they have formed a bit of a ‘skin’ and are a light gold in color – usually 3 – 5 minutes; don’t overcook and let them go beyond a golden brown. Remove the **potatoes** from the oil and set aside to drain on a paper towel lined plate.
3. Now finely chop the **onions**, **garlic**, **ginger**, and **green chile** in a food processor.
4. Heat the oil in a deep cast iron enameled saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the **cumin seeds**. When they begin to sputter, add the **onion mixture**. Fry the mixture, stirring occasionally, until the **onions** are lightly browned, 5 to 10 minutes.
5. If you are using diced tomatoes, blend them in a food processor until smooth, then reduce the heat to medium and add them to the **onion mixture**. If you’re using crushed tomatoes, you can add them directly to the **onions** without blending.
6. Cook the **onion-tomato mixture** for about 5 or 6 minutes, or until all the liquid has evaporated and you can see the oil around the edges.
7. Add the **sour cream** and **yogurt** and cook, stirring continuously, for about 5 minutes. Add all the **spices**, the **salt**, and stir rapidly for about a minute.
8. Add the **chicken thighs**, 1½ cups of hot water or **chicken stock**, and mix well to cover the **chicken** with the sauce. Cover, raise the heat, and bring the mixture to a boil. Reduce the heat to medium-low and cook, stirring occasionally for about 30 minutes, or until the **chicken** is tender. If you are using **potatoes**, add them to the curry about halfway through the 30 minute period, making sure that they are covered by the liquid. Add additional hot water or stock, one tablespoon at a time, during this time if the mixture appears too dry.
