# Description

This venison chili recipe is a hearty and flavorful dish that is perfect for a cold day. It is made with ground venison, vegetables, and a variety of spices for a delicious and satisfying meal.

---
# Ingredients
- [ ] 1# Ground Beef (venison)
- [ ] 1 Medium Onion *chopped*
- [ ] 1/2 Red Bell Pepper *chopped*
- [ ] 1/2 Package Soyrizo
- [ ] 1 package Lipton Onion Soup mix
- [ ] ~1/2 tsp Cumin
- [ ] ~1/2 tsp Garlic powder
- [ ] ~1 Tbs Chili Powder
- [ ] 1/4 tsp Cayenne Pepper
- [ ] 1/4 tsp Black Pepper
- [ ] 1 large can Crushed Tomatoes
- [ ] 1 small can Rotel
- [ ] 2 15oz cans Kidney Beans (rinsed, don't want the starch water)
- [ ] 2 15oz cans Chili Beans (don't rinse you want the bean liquid)

### Optional
- [ ] Sour Cream
- [ ] More Onion
- [ ] Cilantro

---
# Method
1. **Brown ground beef** in a little oil
2. Add **onion**, **peppers**, and **soyrizo** and **sauté** until soft
3. Add **tomatoes**, **rotel**, **beans**, and **seasonings**
4. 1. Simmer for 1 hour. Optionally pressure cook
