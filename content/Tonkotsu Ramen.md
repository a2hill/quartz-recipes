## Description
Makes one bowl
___
## Ingredients
- [ ] 300ml [[Tonkotsu Soup Base]]
- [ ] 45ml [[Bare Bones Shio Tare]] or 30ml [[Derek's Ramen_Lord Tare]]
	- For the pure pork experience use the Bare Bones. For an Umami Experience use Derek’s 
	- This is a rough suggestion. Try a small amount with your broth and dial in the ratio to your taste
- [ ] 15ml [[All Purpose Negi (Scallion) Oil]]
- [ ] 130 g Sun Noodles
    - You can make your own, but its hard. I buy a fresh ramen kit from Sun at the asian grocery store, it comes with 2x 130g servings, and then I throw away the other soup base
- [ ] 1 [[Quick Ramen Egg (Ajitama)]]
- [ ] 1 Slice [[Sous Vide Chashu]]
### Optional Toppings
- [ ] Green onion
- [ ] Fried Garlic
- [ ] Corn
- [ ] Bamboo Shoots
- [ ] Wood ear mushrooms
---
## Method
1. Place your serving bowl in the oven at 180 degrees. You don't want a cold bowl ruining your soup.
2. Put your noodle water on to boil
3. Get your soup hot
4. Determine your Tare to Broth ratio
5. Sear your slice of Chashu. I used a sears-all but you could torch it or use a hot pan or broiler
6. Put your noodles on to boil. Mine were 2:30. Be precise about this, its very important
7. Prep your bowl. First add the scallion oil, then the tare, then the broth
8. When your noodles are done, shake them real good to get off the excess water and then slide them into your bowl. Optionally perform a noodle fold to make them look nice
9. Add egg and other toppings
