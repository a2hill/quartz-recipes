# Description
This Mint Salsa Verde is a refreshing and flavorful condiment that brings a delightful twist to traditional salsa verde!

---
# Ingredients
- [ ] 2 bunches of fresh parsley
- [ ] 1 bunch of fresh mint
- [ ] 2 tbsp salted capers
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves
- [ ] 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- [ ] 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- [ ] 2/3 cup extra virgin olive oil

---
# Method
1. Coarsely chop the **fresh parsley** and **mint**. Place in a food processor.
2. Drain the **capers** and add to the food processor.
3. Add **garlic**, **mustard**, **vinegar**, and **olive oil**, and process until combined and smooth. Season with **salt** and **pepper**.
