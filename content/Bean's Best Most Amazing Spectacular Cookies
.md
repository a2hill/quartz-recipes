# Description
These cookies are so delicious, you'll want to eat them all in one sitting!

---
# Ingredients
- [ ] 2 cups AP Flour
- [ ] 1 package pudding mix (vanilla, cookies n cream, chocolate, etc)
- [ ] 1/2 tsp baking soda
- [ ] 1/2 tsp cream of tartar
- [ ] 3/4 tsp salt
- [ ] 1 cup butter (salted)
- [ ] 1/2 cup brown sugar
- [ ] 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- [ ] 2 eggs
- [ ] 1 tsp vanilla extract
- [ ] 1/2-3/4 cup dark chocolate chunks

---
# Method
1. Combine all dry ingredients (**Flour**, **pudding mix**, **baking soda**, **cream of tartar**, and **salt**) Mix or sift together.
2. Make sure your **butter** is nice and soft and mix in an electric mixer until creamy.
3. Add **sugars** on a lower speed.
4. Then add **eggs** 1 at a time.
5. Finally add the **vanilla extract**.
6. Keep mixer on low and slowly add the dry ingredients.
7. After they are incorporated, add your **chocolate chips**.
8. Cook at 375 for 10 mins or until a golden brown.
9. Burn your mouth and eat immediately.
