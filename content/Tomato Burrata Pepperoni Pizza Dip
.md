# Description
Everything you’d expect from a fancy recipe, but made in way less than an hour and all in one pot too!

---
# Ingredients
- [ ] 2-3 cups cherry tomatoes
- [ ] 4 cloves garlic, chopped
- [ ] 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- [ ] 2 tablespoons chopped fresh oregano ((or 1 tablespoon dried))
- [ ] 1 teaspoon crushed fennel seed
- [ ] kosher salt and black pepper
- [ ] chili flakes
- [ ] 1 cup marinara sauce
- [ ] 8 ounces cream cheese, at room temperature
- [ ] 3/4 cup shredded whole milk mozzarella
- [ ] 3/4 cup shredded provolone
- [ ] 2-3 balls burrata cheese, at room temperature
- [ ] 4-6 ounces pepperoni, sliced
- [ ] lots of fresh basil

---
# Method
1. Preheat oven to 425° F.
2. Place the **burrata** on a kitchen towel and gently wrap it up, let sit to draw out the excess water.
3. In a large baking dish or cast iron skillet, toss together the **olive oil**, **tomatoes**, **garlic**, **oregano**, and **fennel seeds**.
4. Season with **salt**, **pepper**, and **chili flakes**.
5. Bake 15-20 minutes, until the **tomatoes** are bursting.
6. Mix in the **marinara sauce**.
7. Meanwhile, in a bowl, mix the **cream cheese** with the **mozzarella**.
8. Dollop the cheese mix over the **tomatoes**.
9. Sprinkle over the **provolone**, then break the **burrata** over everything, tearing it into pieces over the dip.
10. Layer on the **pepperoni**.
11. Bake 15 minutes, until the cheese is melted.
12. Top with lots of fresh **basil**.
13. Serve with toasted bread.
