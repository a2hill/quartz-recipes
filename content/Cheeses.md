## Mimolette
This traditional cheese from the Normandy region of France has been aged for a minimum of twelve months, and you can certainly see so in its golden-orange, butterscotch hue and beautifully bloomy rind. More importantly, however, you can also _taste_ it in every savory, satisfying, subtly-sweet bite.
