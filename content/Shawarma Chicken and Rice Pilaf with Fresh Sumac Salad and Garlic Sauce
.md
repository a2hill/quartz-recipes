# Description
This recipe for Shawarma Chicken and Rice Pilaf with Fresh Sumac Salad and Garlic Sauce is a simplified version of the classic dish, using Better Than Bouillon Roasted Chicken Base for added flavor.

---

# Method
1. In a bowl, combine the chicken with the **paprika**, **cumin**, **onion powder**, **coriander**, **allspice**, **turmeric**, **pepper**, **salt**, **olive oil**, **garlic**, **lemon juice**, and **Better Than Bouillon Roasted Chicken Base**. Mix well.
Marinate for as long as your patience can handle.
2. Heat a large pan over medium-high heat and add a drizzle of olive oil. Once hot, add the chicken in batches.
Cook for 6 to 8 mins per side until golden and cooked through. Set aside.
3. Reduce heat to medium-low. Add **butter** to the same pan then the **onions** and cook, stirring often, until translucent. Add the **garlic** and spices (**turmeric**, **paprika**, **garlic powder**, **pepper**) and cook for 1 to 2 mins. Add the **rice** and toast for 2 mins while stirring.
Pour in the hot water mixed with **Better Than Bouillon Roasted Chicken Base**.
Stir and bring to a boil. Once boiling, cover and reduce heat to low. Simmer for 20 mins until cooked.
Mix the salad ingredients in one bowl and the sauce ingredients in another bowl. Enjoy!
