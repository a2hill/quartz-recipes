# Ingredients
- [ ] Tuna, 2 cans
- [ ] Green onions
- [ ] Mini cucumbers, 3
- [ ] Red onion, 0.5 large
- [ ] Dill, 2 tbsp
- [ ] Pickles, 8 small
- [ ] Greek yogurt, 1 cup
- [ ] Avocado, 1
- [ ] Mustard, 1 tbsp
- [ ] Pickle juice, 1 tbsp
- [ ] Paprika, 1 tsp
- [ ] Salt, 0.5 tsp

---
# Method
<!-- In this section, any time you reference an ingredient (onions, chicken, cheese, garlic, etc.) be sure to bold it. For example 'Add the garlic' becomes 'Add the **garlic**'. Don't forget about things like **salt** **pepper** and **seasonings** which may not have been listed in the ingredients --> 
1. Drain the **tuna** and place it in a large mixing bowl.
2. Chop the **green onions**, **mini cucumbers**, **red onion**, and **dill** and add them to the bowl.
3. Slice the **pickles** and add them to the bowl.
4. In a separate bowl, mix together the **Greek yogurt**, **avocado**, **mustard**, **pickle juice**, **paprika**, and **salt** to make the dressing.
5. Pour the dressing over the tuna and vegetable mixture and stir until well combined.
