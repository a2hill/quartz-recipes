# Description
This Spicy Asian Coleslaw is a refreshing and flavorful twist on traditional coleslaw. The combination of chipotle, sesame seeds, fish sauce, and fresh herbs creates a unique and delicious dressing that will elevate any meal.

---
# Ingredients
- [ ] 1 chipotle or smoked jalapeno (I usually use about 2 of the canned peppers)
- [ ] 1 cup toasted sesame seeds
- [ ] 1/3 cup fish sauce
- [ ] 2/3 cup rice vinegar
- [ ] 1 T chopped garlic
- [ ] 1 T grated ginger
- [ ] 1 T chopped lemon grass
- [ ] 1 bunch cilantro, chopped
- [ ] 1 T chopped fresh basil
- [ ] 1 T chopped fresh mint
- [ ] Juice of 2 limes
- [ ] 4 cups bagged coleslaw cabbage mix with the carrots in it (you can adjust the amount of cabbage to suit the texture you like)

---
# Method
1. Place all **sauce** ingredients into a blender.
2. Process until thoroughly mixed.
3. Pour over **cabbage**.
4. Mix gently to coat.
