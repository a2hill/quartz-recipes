# Description
Making this classic seafood sauce at home is easy and the results are far better than store-bought options.

---
# Ingredients
- [ ] ¾ cup mayonnaise
- [ ] 2 shallots, minced
- [ ] 2 tablespoons capers, rinsed and minced
- [ ] 2 tablespoons sweet pickle relish
- [ ] 2 teaspoons white vinegar
- [ ] ½ teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- [ ] ½ teaspoon pepper

---
# Method
1. Mix all ingredients together in small bowl.
2. Cover and let sit to blend flavors, about 15 minutes.
3. Stir again before serving.
