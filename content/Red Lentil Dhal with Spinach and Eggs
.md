# Description
This flavorful and nutritious dish combines red lentils, spinach, and eggs in a creamy coconut milk sauce. It's perfect for a quick and easy weeknight meal.

---
# Ingredients
- [ ] 2 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 1 brown onion, finely diced
- [ ] 3 clove garlic, roughly chopped
- [ ] 1½ cup red lentils
- [ ] 2 tbsp tomato paste
- [ ] 2 tbsp curry powder
- [ ] 1.25L vegetable stock
- [ ] 200ml coconut milk
- [ ] 150g baby spinach
- [ ] 4 free range eggs
- [ ] Large handful picked coriander leaves, roughly chopped
- [ ] Low GI wraps, to serve

---
# Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp of olive oil in a large frying pan over medium heat.
2. Add the **onion**, **garlic**, **spinach leaves**, and a good pinch of **salt**.
3. Cook, stirring often for 2-3 minutes, until the leaves are wilted.
4. Add the **tomato paste** and **lentils**, stirring to combine. Cook for another minute.
5. Pour in the **vegetable stock** and cook, stirring often, for 20-25 minutes, until the lentils are creamy and softened.
6. Stir in 2/3 of the **coconut milk** and the **baby spinach**, cooking until the spinach is just wilted. Adjust the seasoning to taste.
7. Crack the 4 **eggs** into the simmering dhal. Cover and cook on low heat for 6-7 minutes, until the eggs are cooked to your liking.
