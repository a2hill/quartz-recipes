# Description
This recipe is for baked stuffed potatoes. It is a delicious side dish that can be made with frozen or fresh potatoes.

---
# Ingredients
- [ ] 5 large baking potatoes
- [ ] 2 tablespoons butter
- [ ] 5 green onions, chopped
- [ ] 1/2 cup butter
- [ ] 1 pint sour cream
- [ ] Milk
- [ ] Salt
- [ ] Pepper
- [ ] Garlic powder
- [ ] Cayenne pepper
- [ ] Parmesan cheese
- [ ] Paprika

---
# Method

Don't forget about things like **salt** **pepper** and **seasonings** which may not have been listed in the ingredients --> 
1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees.
2. Bake the **potatoes** for about 1 hour or until they are soft enough to squeeze.
3. In a pan, saute the **green onions** in 2 tablespoons of **butter**.
4. Slice off the top of each **potato** and scoop out the insides into a bowl.
5. Using an electric mixer or potato masher, whip the **potato** with 1/2 cup of **butter**, 1 pint of **sour cream**, and the sauteed **onions**.
6. Add a little **milk** to achieve a softer texture.
7. Season with **salt**, **pepper**, **garlic powder**, and a touch of **cayenne pepper** to taste.
8. Spoon the mixture back into the empty **potato** shells.
9. If desired, refrigerate at this point.
10. Sprinkle with **Parmesan cheese** and **paprika**.
11. Place the potatoes on a cookie sheet and bake at 350 degrees for about 20 minutes, or until heated through and the top is lightly browned.
