# Description
This refreshing and flavorful salad is perfect for a hot summer day. The cucumber noodles are coated in a delicious peanut gochujang sauce, making it a unique and tasty dish.

---
# Ingredients
- [ ] 2 Cucumbers
- [ ] 2 tbsp smooth peanut butter (sub for tahini)
- [ ] 1 tbsp gochujang paste
- [ ] 1 tsp minced garlic
- [ ] 1 tsp chili garlic crisp
- [ ] 1 1/2 tsp soy sauce
- [ ] 1 tbsp honey
- [ ] 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- [ ] 2-3 tbsp hot water
- [ ] 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- [ ] 4 green onions, thinly sliced
- [ ] 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds

---
# Method
1. In a serving bowl, combine the **peanut butter**, **gochujang**, **soy sauce**, **sugar**, **hot water**, **chili garlic crisp**, **garlic**, and **rice vinegar**. Set aside.
2. Make the cucumber noodles using a julienne peeler or vegetable spiral slicer. Add the cucumber noodles to the peanut dressing and add the **sesame oil**, stirring to coat the noodles. Top with **green onion** and **toasted sesame seeds**.
