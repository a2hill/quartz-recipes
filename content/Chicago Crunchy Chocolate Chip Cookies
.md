# Description
These Chicago Crunchy Chocolate Chip Cookies are a delicious treat that are perfect for satisfying your sweet tooth. They are low in cholesterol, trans-fat free, low carb, and low sodium, making them a healthier option for indulging in a sweet treat.

---
# Ingredients
- [ ] 1 cup butter
- [ ] 1 cup sugar
- [ ] 1 cup brown sugar
- [ ] 1 large egg
- [ ] 1 tablespoon milk
- [ ] 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- [ ] 3 ½ cups all-purpose flour
- [ ] 1 teaspoon salt
- [ ] 3 teaspoons baking powder
- [ ] 1 cup vegetable oil
- [ ] 1 cup quick cooking oats
- [ ] 1 cup corn flakes
- [ ] 12 ounces chocolate chips

---
# Method
1. In a large mixing bowl, **cream butter** with **sugar** and **brown sugar**.
2. Add **egg**, **milk**, and **vanilla extract** to the mixture.
3. In a separate bowl, combine **flour**, **salt**, and **baking powder**.
4. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, alternating with **vegetable oil**.
5. Stir in **oats**, **corn flakes**, and **chocolate chips**.
6. Drop the cookie dough by teaspoonful onto ungreased cookie sheets.
7. Bake at 350°F (180°C) for 10 to 13 minutes.
8. Let the cookies cool before serving.
9. Enjoy your delicious Chicago Crunchy Chocolate Chip Cookies!
