# Description

This zesty pasta salad is perfect for a summer BBQ or potluck. It's packed with fresh ingredients and a tangy dressing that will have everyone coming back for seconds.

---
# Ingredients
- [ ] 2-3 boxes rotini noodles
- [ ] 1 block feta cheese, crumbled
- [ ] 1 bunch cilantro, chopped
- [ ] 1 cup green olives, sliced
- [ ] 1 bottle zesty Italian dressing
- [ ] 1 jar artichoke hearts, drained and chopped
- [ ] 1 small red onion, diced
- [ ] 1 container mini mozzarella balls
- [ ] 2 tomatoes, diced
- [ ] Optional: A splash of rice vinegar (for extra tang)

---
# Method
1. **Cook the pasta**
Boil rotini noodles according to the package instructions. Drain and rinse with cold water to cool.
2. **Prep the ingredients**
While the pasta cooks, chop all other ingredients: **cilantro**, **olives**, **artichoke hearts**, **red onion**, and **tomatoes**.
3. **Combine**
In a large mixing bowl, toss the cooled pasta with chopped ingredients, **feta**, and **mozzarella balls**.
4. **Dress it up**
Pour in the bottle of **zesty Italian dressing** and add a splash of **rice vinegar** if desired. Mix until evenly coated.
5. **Chill and serve**
