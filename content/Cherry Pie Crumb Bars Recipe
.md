# Description
Cherry Pie Crumb Bars are quick and easy crumb bars with fresh cherry filling. Buttery crumb topping and sweet fruit filling make this a perfect summer dessert.

---
# Ingredients
- [ ] ½ cup unsalted butter melted, cooled to room temperature
- [ ] ½ cup granulated sugar
- [ ] 1½ cup all-purpose flour
- [ ] ½ teaspoon baking soda
- [ ] ½ teaspoon baking powder
- [ ] ¼ teaspoon salt
- [ ] 2 cups fresh cherries, pitted, each sliced in half
- [ ] 1 Tablespoon cornstarch
- [ ] 1 Tablespoon lemon juice
- [ ] 1 teaspoon granulated sugar

---
# Method
1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. Line a square 8" baking pan with parchment paper.
Butter the sides and bottom of lined pan. Set aside.
3. In a medium mixing bowl, stir together **cherries**, **cornstarch** and **lemon juice**.
4. In a large mixing bowl, stir together melted **butter** and **sugar**.
5. Add **flour**, **baking soda**, **baking powder** and **salt** and stir with a fork until the mixture resembles crumbs.
6. Reserve ¾ cup of mixture.
7. Press remaining crumb mixture into the bottom of prepared pan.
8. Spread **cherry filling** over the crust.
9. Sprinkle remaining crumb mixture over cherries.
10. Sprinkle **sugar** over crumb topping.
11. Bake bars for 23 to 25 minutes OR until the top is golden.
