# Ingredients
- [ ] Ground beef 20-30% fat
- [ ] Parsley
- [ ] Minced onion
- [ ] Salt
- [ ] Pepper

# Method
<!-- In this section, any time you reference an ingredient (onions, chicken, cheese, garlic, etc.) be sure to bold it. Don't forget about things like **salt** **pepper** and **seasonings** which may not have been listed in the ingredients --> 
1. Mince the **onion** in the food processor, strain in a strainer and save the onion juice for later.
2. Mix all ingredients together very well, add **seasoning** to your taste.
3. Shape the meat on screw wet hand with onion juice and dab the beef.
4. Brush onion juice as you barbecue to keep the meat moist.
