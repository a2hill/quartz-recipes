# Description
This Spinach Lasagna is a delicious and comforting dish that is perfect for any occasion. Layers of oven-ready noodles, spinach ricotta filling, and a creamy white garlic sauce make for a flavorful and satisfying meal.

---
# Ingredients
- [ ] 1 box oven-ready lasagna noodles
- [ ] 6 cups shredded mozzarella, for layering & topping
- [ ] 12oz frozen chopped spinach, thawed & squeezed dry
- [ ] 30 oz ricotta cheese
- [ ] 2 large eggs
- [ ] 2 cups shredded mozzarella
- [ ] 1 cup grated Parmesan
- [ ] 1 tsp onion powder
- [ ] 1 tsp garlic salt
- [ ] 1 tsp black pepper black pepper to taste
- [ ] 4 tbsp butter
- [ ] 10 cloves garlic, minced
- [ ] 2 cups heavy cream
- [ ] 2 cups whole milk
- [ ] 2 cups grated Parmesan
- [ ] 2 tsp garlic powder
- [ ] 1 tsp onion powder
- [ ] Salt & black pepper to taste

---
# Method
1. Melt the **butter** over medium-low heat. Add the **garlic** and cook for about 2-3 minutes until fragrant.
2. Pour in the **heavy cream** and **milk**. Add **garlic powder**, **onion powder**, **salt** and **pepper**. Gently simmer for 3-5 minutes.
3. Stir in the **Parmesan** until melted and the white garlic sauce is smooth and creamy. Remove from heat.
4. In a large bowl, combine the **spinach**, **ricotta**, **eggs**, **mozzarella**, **Parmesan**, **onion powder**, **garlic salt** and **pepper**.
5. Spread a layer of white garlic sauce in the bottom of your baking dish.
6. Layer oven-ready noodles, spinach ricotta filling, noodles, white garlic sauce, noodles. Repeat until all your layers are built.
7. Finish with plenty of white garlic sauce and **mozzarella** on top layer!
8. Cover tightly with foil and bake at 375°F for 45 minutes.
9. Remove the foil and bake another 15-20 minutes until golden and bubbly.
10. Broil on LOW for a couple minutes at the end if needed for extra color.
