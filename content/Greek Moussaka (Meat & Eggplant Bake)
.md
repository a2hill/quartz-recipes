# Description
A classic Greek comfort dish featuring layers of roasted potatoes and eggplant, a rich beef ragu, and a silky olive oil béchamel baked until deeply golden.

---
# Ingredients
- [ ] 1 brown onion, roughly diced
- [ ] 1 red onion, roughly diced
- [ ] 3–5 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 1 kg ground beef
- [ ] 2 tbsp dried oregano
- [ ] 2 tbsp paprika
- [ ] 1 tbsp ground cinnamon
- [ ] 1 cup red wine
- [ ] 800 g crushed tomatoes
- [ ] 3 bay leaves
- [ ] Extra virgin olive oil
- [ ] Salt & black pepper
- [ ] 10 small potatoes (or 4 large)
- [ ] 3 medium-large eggplants
- [ ] Extra virgin olive oil
- [ ] Salt
- [ ] 100 mL extra virgin olive oil
- [ ] 100 g all-purpose flour
- [ ] 1 L whole milk
- [ ] 2 eggs
- [ ] 2 egg yolks
- [ ] ¼ whole nutmeg, grated
- [ ] 100 g Parmesan or Grana Padano
- [ ] Salt

# Method

1. Make the Ragu
Cook **onions** in **olive oil** with a pinch of **salt** for 8–10 minutes. Add **garlic** for 1 minute. Brown the **beef**, stir in the spices, deglaze with **wine**, add **tomatoes** and **bay leaves**, then simmer 60–90 minutes until thick.
2. Roast the Vegetables
Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Roast sliced **potatoes** for 30 minutes. Salt the **eggplant**, pat dry, then roast for 25 minutes until lightly browned.
3. Make the Béchamel
Cook **olive oil** and **flour** for 1 minute. Gradually whisk in **milk** until slightly thickened. Remove from heat and quickly whisk in **eggs**, **yolks**, **nutmeg**, **cheese**, and **salt**.
4. Assemble
Layer **potatoes**, **eggplant**, **beef ragu**, then spread the **béchamel** evenly over the top.
5. Bake
Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 40–45 minutes. Broil briefly if needed for a richly browned top.
6. Rest
Allow to rest for at least 30 minutes before serving.
