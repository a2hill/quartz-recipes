# Description
This Shrimp Pasta Salad is a delicious and easy dish that is perfect for a summer cookout or potluck. It features tender shrimp, hard-boiled eggs, and a creamy dressing with a kick of heat. 

---
# Ingredients
- [ ] 1 box small shell pasta
- [ ] 3 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
- [ ] ½ red onion, finely chopped
- [ ] Medium raw shrimp (as much as you like)
- [ ] Mayonnaise
- [ ] Cocktail sauce (or a mix of ketchup and horseradish)
- [ ] Optional: hot sauce for heat
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

---
# Method
<!-- In this section, any time you reference an ingredient (onions, chicken, cheese, garlic, etc.) be sure to bold it. For example 'Add the garlic' becomes 'Add the **garlic**'. Don't forget about things like **salt** **pepper** and **seasonings** which may not have been listed in the ingredients --> 
1. Cook the **pasta** according to package instructions. Drain and let cool.
2. Boil the **eggs**, peel, and chop.
3. Boil the **shrimp** until just cooked (don’t overcook!). Drain, peel if needed, and let cool.
4. In a large bowl, combine **pasta**, **eggs**, **shrimp**, and **red onion**.
5. Add **mayonnaise** and **cocktail sauce** (or your preferred mix) to taste. Adjust based on how creamy you like it.
6. Season with **salt**, **pepper**, and **hot sauce** if using.
